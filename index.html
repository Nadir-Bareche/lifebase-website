Questions

Do I need Excel, or does Google Sheets work?
Both. LifeBase is a single .xlsx file — open it in Excel 2016 or newer, or upload it straight into Google Sheets.
Is this a subscription?
No. It's a single one-time purchase. The file is yours to keep and use for as long as you want.
What does "single-user license" mean?
Your purchase is for your own personal use on your own devices. If someone else on your team or in your family wants to use LifeBase, they're welcome to grab their own copy.
I'm not a spreadsheet person — is this hard to use?
No formulas to write, ever. You only type into the white cells; everything grey calculates itself. The built-in "Start Here" guide walks you through your first five minutes.
What if I need help after buying?
Every download includes step-by-step PDF guides covering setup, your weekly routine, and troubleshooting tips.
